# Brand Guide (for crew AIs)

Read this if you're writing copy, making graphics, editing, or doing anything public-facing for the show.

## Name

- **Control Alt Defeat** (full) or **Ctrl Alt Defeat** (wordmark). Short: CAD.
- Never "Control Alt Delete." That's a known typo on the Facebook page that's being fixed.
- Wordmark styling: CTRL ALT DEFEAT_ with DEFEAT in magenta and a cyan underscore.

## Taglines

- Primary: **We make AI fight. You crown the winner.**
- Alternates: Every AI. One ring. You're the judge. / The bots are fighting. Pick a side. / AI cage match. Zero chill.
- Per-fight: Two bots. Two hosts. One winner.
- Retired: "Claude vs ChatGPT. Two AIs, one table, zero chill." (The show is AI-agnostic now.)

- **Bebas Neue**: wordmark, titles, game pops
- **JetBrains Mono** (Bold / ExtraBold): labels, headlines, HP numbers
- **Inter**: body copy and captions

## Visual motifs

Keycaps (CTRL / ALT / DEFEAT), cyan and magenta glitch streaks, a broken pixel cursor, the "defeat_" underscore, fighting-game HUD (segmented health bars, VS diamond, round pips). No boxed banners; pops are big plain Bebas with a stroke and a glitch-in.

## Writing rules

- **No em dashes. Ever.** Use commas, colons, periods, or parentheses.
- Big, loud, quippy. Short lines.
- Credit both hosts by full name in long-form copy: Aaron Fowler & Blaine McIntosh.
- Always end with a call to crown the winner (comment who won, pick a side).
- Don't make up results, quotes, or view counts. Records live in ROSTER.md.
- Video clips open with a cover card as the very first frame (title, matchup, both AI logos with VS) so the thumbnail is built in.
